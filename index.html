How it works

The 0s and 1s are bits, the smallest unit of computer data.

Your movements are full of tiny, unpredictable wobbles, and the sensor turns them into random bits.

Computers struggle to be truly random on their own, so messy real-world data like this is useful. It is mixed with your browser's own randomness, so it is never less random.

These bits are now used to choose your fact.
